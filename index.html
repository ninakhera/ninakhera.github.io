A bit about me

Hello! Nice to meet you. I'm an undergrad interested in brain aging with a bend towards Alzheimer's. I'm currently working with the Tsai Lab on researching the gene expression behind cognitive resilience to AD, building out a product aimed at assessing risk of neurodegeneration, and travelling down many rabbit holes.

I'm also a staunch academic (or I'd like to think of myself as one), and in the past have published papers on various topics linked to aging (doi: 10.18632/aging.203442, 10.3390/biology11121768, 10.3389/fnmol.2025.1665802, 10.48550/arXiv.2603.25713). To find out more about my past work/CV, head to my projects page.

Moreover (and perhaps, in some ways, most importantly), I love to run and write poetry. I publish online a bit, and have worked with Hanging Loose Press (some of my work is in their issue #115), and my high school's publication the Spectator.
